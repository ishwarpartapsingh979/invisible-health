# My 6-Day Meal Plan (Mon–Sat)

**Goal:** Fat loss + muscle (recomp). Protein at every meal, moderate calories, whole grains, minimal oil, lots of veg & greens.

**Chef principles baked in:**
- A different dal/legume almost every day → gut & amino-acid diversity
- Leafy greens rotated all week (palak, methi, sarson) + cruciferous (broccoli, cabbage, gobhi) + gourds (lauki, turai) — the stuff usually missed
- Fermented food + curd daily → probiotics
- Omega-3 from salmon + flax; iron from greens (add lemon to help absorption)
- Grill / **air-fry** / sauté / bake over frying; cold-pressed oil, used sparingly

---

## THE LEAN-UP PRINCIPLE (apply to EVERY meal)
The egg move — *3 whites + 1 yolk* — is one example of one rule:
**keep or boost the protein; strip the hidden fat / refined carb / calorie load that adds nothing.**
Apply the same logic everywhere:

- **Paneer →** use **toned / double-toned milk paneer** (leaner), or do **half paneer + half tofu**. Grill or sauté, never fry. In palak paneer: more palak, less paneer, **no cream**.
- **Curd / yogurt →** choose **low-fat or hung/Greek curd** → more protein, less fat per spoon. (Same idea as egg whites.)
- **Chicken →** **skinless breast** (already the lean cut); curry with less oil, **no cream/malai**, tomato-onion base instead.
- **Paneer tikka / marinades →** marinate in **low-fat hung curd + spices**, not cream.
- **Dal →** keep the dal, **kill the heavy tadka** — 1 tsp oil/ghee max, no cream. Lighter dals (moong, masoor) over heavier (urad, chana) on cut days.
- **Rajma / lobia / chole / soya →** light gravy, **measured oil**, squeeze soaked soya chunks well so they don't soak oil. Pair the higher-carb ones (rajma/chole) with a **smaller rice portion**.
- **Rice →** smaller portion + **more dal/protein to fill the plate**; or stretch it with **cauliflower rice / extra veg**; brown/hand-pounded over white. Cook-cool-reheat for resistant starch.
- **Roti / parantha →** **mix besan or moong dal flour into the atta** = more protein per roti (the bread version of "egg whites"). Cook paranthas on **minimal oil / dry-roast**, not ghee-loaded.
- **Cheela / chilla / dosa →** **brush** oil on the pan, don't pour; bulk with extra veg/paneer for more protein.
- **Milk (pancakes/coffee) →** **toned / skimmed** instead of full-fat.
- **Chutney / dressing →** **mint-coriander or lemon + hung curd** over coconut/cream/mayo (coconut chutney = small portion only).
- **Oil, universally →** **measure it (1 tsp/dish), don't free-pour.** This single habit is the biggest hidden-calorie fix.

**One-line test before cooking any dish:** *"Can I keep the protein and taste but make it leaner — leaner cut, less oil, less cream, smaller refined carb, more veg?"* If yes, do it.

### 🔥 Air fryer = your lean-up power tool
Use it to get the "fried/tandoori" taste with little or no oil. Best uses:
- **Proteins:** paneer tikka, tofu, chicken breast/tikka, fish/salmon — marinate in hung curd + spices, air-fry (no oil pool)
- **Veg:** bhindi (air-fried instead of oily fried!), gobhi, broccoli, capsicum, baingan — toss with ½ tsp oil + spices
- **Snacks:** roast your own makhana, chana, nuts; air-fry sweet potato
- **Rule:** a light oil *spray/brush* is all it needs — never a pour

## DAILY NON-NEGOTIABLES (every day, on top of meals)
- **Curd or raita** with lunch (probiotic)
- **Big raw salad / kachumber** with lunch & dinner (cucumber, tomato, onion, carrot, beet, lemon)
- **3 L water**; lemon water on waking
- **Mid-meal snacks (you have ~2 yogurts/day):** Epigamia Turbo 15g (flavoured + Greek mix) + berries/fruit — your main snack ×2/day • OR small handful **walnuts or almonds (~15–20g)**, walnuts best for triglycerides • OR roasted/air-fried **makhana** • OR **moong sprouts chaat**. *(No roasted chana.)*
- **Yogurt & fruit limits:** yogurt 2–3 servings/day = fine (it's protein). **Fruit ~2 modest servings/day** (½ cup / small handful each), mostly **berries/guava/papaya/apple**; limit mango/banana/grapes/chikoo. Always whole, never juiced, no honey/granola added.
- Dinner **2–3 hrs before sleep** (helps your sleep score)
- **Caffeine cut-off: 2 pm.** Morning coffee/tea only. After 2 pm → caffeine-free (herbal tea, not green tea/coffee). Protects your short sleep (a key WHOOP driver).
- **Nothing after dinner** (no bedtime milk — your preference; dinner protein covers recovery).
- **Egg standard:** whenever eggs are the breakfast protein → **3 egg whites + 1 whole egg** (omelette or bhurji)
- **ABC juice (apple-beet-carrot), breakfast:** genuinely good micronutrients — BUT juicing strips the fibre and concentrates sugar. *Lean-up move:* **blend it, don't juice it** (keep the pulp/fibre → blunts the sugar spike), keep the portion to **one small glass**, and have it **alongside your protein**, not as a standalone sugary hit. Count it as ~80–120 cal.
- **Coconut water, daily:** fine — low-cal (~45 cal/glass), great electrolytes, especially on training/sweat days. Just count it (it does have natural sugar) and don't treat it as unlimited — 1 glass/day is the sweet spot.

## 🏋️ EVENING WORKOUT STRUCTURE (you train evenings, pre-dinner)
This is built to **feed muscle (your #1 WHOOP driver) while protecting sleep (your #2)** — all from natural food, no powders.

**Pre-workout (~45–60 min before training):** light, natural carb + a little protein, easy to digest. Pick one:
- 1 banana + 4–5 almonds/walnuts
- Small bowl fruit + 2 tbsp curd
- 2 dates + coconut water
- Small bowl poha/oats (if you trained later/hungrier)
- *Avoid:* heavy, oily, or very high-fibre food right before training
- *No caffeine* (it's after 2 pm)

**Post-workout = DINNER (eat within ~45–60 min of finishing):**
- Make it **protein-forward + light** — this is your recovery meal, so protein is non-negotiable, but keep it light on carbs/fat so it doubles as the lighter dinner your wife wants and doesn't disturb sleep.
- **Carb at dinner is optional and mainly for you:** you (post-workout) can add a small roti / small rice / small quinoa for recovery; the lighter version (wife) skips or halves the carb and keeps protein + veg.
- Aim to finish **2–3 hrs before bed**.

**After dinner:** nothing (no bedtime milk — your dinner protein covers overnight recovery).

> Net effect for your WHOOP: protein hits around training when muscle-building is highest, dinner stays light and early, caffeine is long gone → lean mass up, sleep protected.
