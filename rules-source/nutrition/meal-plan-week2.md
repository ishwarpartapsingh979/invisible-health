# 🗓️ Meal Plan — Week 2 (Mon Jun 29 – Sat Jul 4)

**Goal:** Fat loss + muscle (recomp) · high protein · moderate calories · low sugar/refined carbs · mind triglycerides + fatty liver
*(weights = PER PERSON, pre-cooking/raw, measured while preparing — double for two; tsp ≈ 5g/5ml oil)*

## ✅ Learnings from Week 1 — baked into this plan
1. **Protein at EVERY breakfast** (the #1 fix — crash days last week started on ABC-juice-only).
2. **No punitive light days.** Eat moderate, protein-rich, *consistent* days. Balance across the week, not by starving the day after a heavy one.
3. **Fuel training days fully** — pre-workout snack is non-negotiable; never train depleted.
4. **Train earlier** (morning or early evening, NOT 9 pm) — protects your short sleep, a key WHOOP driver.
5. **Tighten daily sugar** — berries/papaya as default fruit (not mango), ABC small & blended, no honey, watch cumulative ABC + fruit + coconut water.

## Standing rules (unchanged)
- 3 non-veg days (Mon, Thu chicken · Sat salmon) + 3 veg days (Tue, Wed, Fri)
- Egg breakfasts = **3 egg whites + 1 whole egg** · toned-milk paneer only · measured oil (1 tsp/dish) · air-fry where possible · no cream
- 2 rotis each at dinner (whole-grain) · kachumber + curd with meals
- Yogurt ×2/day + fruit (berries/papaya/guava/apple/pear — modest) · snacks: Turbo+fruit / walnuts-almonds / makhana / sprouts (no roasted chana)
- No caffeine after 2 pm · nothing after dinner · coconut water 1 glass/day
- Veg-day dinners: + 1 Epigamia Turbo 15g post-workout for protein
- **Pre-workout (~30–45 min before):** 1 banana + 4–5 almonds, or dates + coconut water
