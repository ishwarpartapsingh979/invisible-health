# My Eating Guide

**Goal:** Lose weight by losing fat AND building muscle (body recomposition).
Not bulking. Not crash dieting.

**The 3 levers that make it work:**
1. Protein HIGH at every meal (protects + builds muscle while losing fat)
2. Calories MODERATE — slight deficit, enough to train & recover, not a surplus
3. Avoid: sugar, maida, fried, excess oil (empty calories = fat, not muscle)

---

## The "Is this OK?" 2-check rule

Before eating anything, run it through both:

1. **Is the base a refined carb?** (maida, white bread, cornflour, big white-rice loads) → if yes, "no sugar" does NOT rescue it.
2. **Is it fried?** → if yes, "no sugar" does NOT rescue it.

"No added sugar" only means something if it ALSO passes these two.
- No-sugar **millet** pancakes → pass ✅
- No-sugar **maida** cookies → fail (refined base) ❌
- No-sugar **fried** banana chips → fail (fried) ❌
- **Fruit juice** (even unsweetened) → fail in spirit (liquid sugar, no fibre) ❌
- Whole fruit (small portion) → pass ✅

## My meal slots (nutritionist plan + what I've vetted)

These combine the nutritionist's "Meal Options" with the products checked in this chat.

### Early morning (optional)
- Warm water + lemon
- Black coffee / green tea (unsweetened)
- Soaked almonds/walnuts or soaked chia water

### Breakfast (protein-anchored — my strongest lever)
Pick ONE, always with protein:
- **Eggs (2–3, minimal oil) + 1 slice whole-grain toast** — my reliable default
- **Mille millet pancakes (made with milk) + 2 eggs** — best combo: plant protein + complete protein, ~30–35g protein, ~400–450 cal. Top with yogurt/berries/thin nut butter — NOT syrup/honey
- **Veg oats upma / besan or moong dal cheela / vegetable uttapam + eggs/paneer/tofu**
- **Greek yogurt (Epigamia plain) + nuts + flax/chia + fruit**
- Dining out: **Greek omelette / shakshuka (light oil) + boiled chicken side** + cortado/black coffee

### Mid-morning (optional)
- Seasonal whole fruit (not juice)
- Buttermilk / chaas
- Coconut water + handful of seeds

### Lunch (build the plate: protein + veg + smaller carb)
- Multigrain roti + dal + sabzi + salad
- Brown rice / quinoa + rajma/chole + cucumber-tomato salad
- Grilled chicken / fish / tandoori paneer / tofu + stir-fried veg + small rice/roti
- Add low-fat curd
- *Tip: protein first, fill half the plate with veg/salad, keep the carb small*

### Evening snack (this is where cravings hit — see craving toolkit below)
- Green/herbal tea or black coffee
- Roasted chana / makhana / groundnuts
- Sprouts chaat
- Clear veg / lentil soup

### Dinner (lighter, 2–3 hrs before sleep — helps my short sleep)
- Grilled paneer/tofu + sautéed veg + 1 roti
- Chicken/fish curry (low oil) + steamed veg + small rice/roti
- Dal + sabzi + salad (skip heavy carbs if eating late)

### Before bed (optional)
- Warm turmeric milk (low-fat)
- Herbal tea
- Small handful walnuts/flaxseeds

## My craving toolkit (vetted in this chat, ranked for fat loss)

### Sweet / dessert craving
1. **Epigamia Turbo flavoured yogurt (15g protein, ~100 cal, stevia-sweetened)** — #1 pick. Best protein-per-calorie of everything. Add berries to make it dessert-like.
2. **Whole Truth MINI bar (Double Cocoa, ~120–130 cal)** — clean (dates-sweetened), portion-controlled. Better than the full-size 52g bar for a cut.
3. **85% dark chocolate** — a square or two, ~50 cal, kills a chocolate craving.

### Crunchy / salty craving
- Roasted makhana, roasted chana

### "I need protein, low calorie"
- **Plain whey scoop in water** (~25g protein, ~120 cal) — best macros, not a craving fix
- **Epigamia plain Turbo (17g protein)** or boiled eggs or paneer cubes

### Bars — verdict
- **The Whole Truth (full 52g):** cleanest bar, but ~250 cal for ~13g protein → treat as occasional clean treat, NOT a protein strategy. Mini version preferred.
- **RiteBite Max Protein 30g:** more protein but sweetened with maltitol (sugar alcohol) → can cause bloating, ~360–400 cal. Emergency/travel only.
- **Yoga Bar / True Elements / Mojo "energy bars":** low protein, date/fig sugar → skip for fat loss.
